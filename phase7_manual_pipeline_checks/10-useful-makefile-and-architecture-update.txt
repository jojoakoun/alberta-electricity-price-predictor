===== USEFUL MAKEFILE HELP =====

WattWise — validated daily commands

  make -f Makefile.useful verify
      Validate configuration, Python, API, frontend, lint, and build.

  make -f Makefile.useful historical-data
      Refresh clean AESO history and validate its data quality.

  make -f Makefile.useful modeling-data
      Rebuild clean history, features, and the training dataset.

  make -f Makefile.useful database-history
      Refresh clean history and synchronize hourly_prices in PostgreSQL.

  make -f Makefile.useful application
      Start Express and React locally; press Ctrl+C to stop both.

  Modeling and decision-policy commands are intentionally withheld
  until the Data Science readiness audit is fully validated.
